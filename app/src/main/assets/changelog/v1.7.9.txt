FEDORATUBE CHANGE LOG
VERSION: 1.7.9
DATE: 2026-11-14
STATUS: PRE-RELEASE

NEW FEATURES
- Introduced FedoraEngine 2.0 recommendation engine powered by user region, watch history, search history, and personal interests
- Added regional discovery & trending blend tailored to your selected country/region
- Integrated recent search history signals directly into Home feed suggestions
- Full Android 17 (API 37) support with edge-to-edge UI and modern system bar integration

UI AND STYLE ENHANCEMENTS
- Complete modern YouTube-style UI redesign across Home, Shorts, Subscriptions, Music, Library, Search, and Video Player
- Added FedoraTube branded header with logo icon and wordmark across top bars
- Added fluid spring animations, rounded thumbnails, press-scale micro-interactions, and smooth category chip transitions
- Optimized APK size to stay well under 25 MB across all architectures
- Hardened ad-free video and music playback with enhanced InnerTube ad-slot filtering
